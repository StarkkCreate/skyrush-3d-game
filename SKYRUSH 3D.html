HOW TO PLAY

Steer with A / D or the arrow keys. Hold W to push the pace, S to ease off, and Shift to boost. Press Space to lift into flight mode and clear ground barriers; land again before the flight meter empties. Drones fill the sky, so stay low when they appear. Collect glowing coins, earn distance bonuses, and spend them in the garage. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons. Esc pauses.
